The Person Behind the Brand

Florence Chioma — “Floxxy”

Florence Chioma, warmly known as Floxxy, is the founder of C.Zara Enterprise. Based in Kurudu, FCT, Abuja, she built the business around a simple belief: families deserve access to wholesome food they can feel good about.

C.Zara began with a personal commitment to carefully sourced honey and quality nuts. The business has grown through customer relationships and a continued focus on quality, care, and personal service.

Today, C.Zara serves households and businesses in Abuja and beyond with natural honey, nuts, palm oil, foodstuffs, and gift selections.
